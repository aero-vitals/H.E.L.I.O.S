PROJECT OVERVIEW
==================================================
H.E.L.I.O.S. (Health Evaluation & Longevity Intelligence for Orbiting Specialists) is an autonomous, 100% offline-first health monitoring and decision-support platform engineered for deep-space exploration and interplanetary missions.
On journeys to the Moon and Mars, radio signals take up to 24 minutes one-way (48 minutes round-trip), rendering real-time guidance from Earth Mission Control impossible during critical emergencies. HELIOS bridges this isolation by transforming the spacecraft into an autonomous health sentry.
Powered by the Helix-Ai Analyst, HELIOS continuously evaluates multi-astronaut vital signs, cabin life-support atmospheric telemetry, and 119 authentic NASA OSDR laboratory biomarkers. It detects physiological anomalies before overt symptoms emerge, visualizes organ-level risks on an interactive 3D Holographic Digital Bio-Twin, and delivers sub-second, spoken voice directives with authentic NASA Quindar tones to safeguard crew health without requiring Earth connectivity.

SCIENTIFIC DATA GROUNDING (NASA OSDR & STANDARDS)
==================================================
1. NASA OSDR OSD-575: Inspiration4 (SOMA) Longitudinal Multi-Omics Profiling (Grounded 119 point-of-care laboratory biomarkers across metabolic, cardiovascular acute-phase, and 71 cytokine panels for spaceflight baseline distributions)
   https://osdr.nasa.gov/bio/repo/data/studies/OSD-575
2. NASA OSDR OSD-569: Whole Blood Measurements from the SpaceX Inspiration4 Mission (Complete Blood Count CBC reference for blood-health indicators and mission telemetry)
   https://osdr.nasa.gov/bio/repo/data/studies/OSD-569
3. NASA-STD-3001: Spaceflight Human-System Standard (Volume 1: Crew Health & Volume 2: Human Factors, Habitability, and Environmental Health)
   https://www.nasa.gov/ochmo/human-spaceflight-and-aviation-standards/
4. NASA Human Research Program (HRP) / Hazard: Gravity Fields (1-1.5% monthly bone mineral density loss in microgravity)
   https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/hazard-gravity-fields/
5. NASA Cardiovascular and Vision / Fluid Shifts Study (~2 liters fluid shift toward the head in microgravity)
   https://www.nasa.gov/directorates/esdmd/hhp/cardiovascular-and-vision/
6. NASA Human Research Program: Risk of Spaceflight Associated Neuro-Ocular Syndrome SANS (~70% long-duration astronauts experiencing SANS signs)
   https://www.nasa.gov/reference/risk-of-spaceflight-associated-neuro-ocular-syndrome-sans/
7. NASA Visible Earth: Blue Marble and Black Marble Global Earth Imagery (Textures for 3D ISS Cupola visualization)
   https://visibleearth.nasa.gov/
